Paytable

Wins pay on the centre line by matching symbols across the 4 reels. 4 of a kind = jackpot, 3 of a kind = solid win, 2 cherries = small win.

NUDGE — randomly awarded after a blank spin. Move any reel up/down one symbol at a time to line up a win.
HOLD — randomly offered before a spin. Lock reels you like so they don't spin next time.
